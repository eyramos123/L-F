How It Works

Reconnecting lost assets is a simple four-step moderation system structured to guarantee speed, safety, and legitimacy.

1. Submit Report

Fill out details about your lost item, or document a found item with maps coordinates.

2. Verification

Administrators review and moderate new posts to prevent spam or false reports.

3. Match Search

Browse details and filter color, locations, categories, or check auto-suggestions.

4. Claim & Return

Contact the reporter securely, verify ownership details, and return the item.
